# Modular-1

**Wake up honey a new FM-1 FW just dropped!**

100% vibecoded FM-1 FW that turns your favorite Chinese synth into a modular synth. 

In the webapp you can build your own patch and uplod it (up to 32) to your FM-1

It's even more Alpha than Andrew Taint at the momenr, there's neither a sequencer nor an arpeggiator nor polyphony, but try it out.
